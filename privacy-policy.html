CutLab AI Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 4, 2026

CutLab AI helps creators turn long videos into short vertical clips. This policy explains what information is processed when you use the application and its TikTok integration.

Information we process

  • Videos and audio you choose to edit or upload.
  • Captions, titles, hashtags and other publishing metadata you provide.
  • TikTok account authorization data, including access tokens and account identifier, only when you connect TikTok.
  • Basic technical information needed to operate the application and report errors.

How we use information

We use this information to edit videos, generate captions, connect your authorized TikTok account and send content to TikTok at your explicit request. We do not sell personal information or use TikTok data for advertising.

TikTok data

CutLab AI uses TikTok's Content Posting API. We request only the permissions needed for the selected feature. Your TikTok password is never collected. You may revoke access at any time from TikTok settings or by disconnecting the account in CutLab AI.

Storage and deletion

Video files and authorization data are stored locally on the computer running CutLab AI unless you explicitly use an external service. You can delete local videos, metadata and saved authorization files at any time.

Changes

We may update this policy when the application or its integrations change. The updated date above will be revised when that happens.
